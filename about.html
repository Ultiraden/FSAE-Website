About Highlander Racing

What is Formula SAE?

Formula SAE is an international competition for student teams to design, manufacture, and race formula-style cars. Each year we plan, finance, and build a bespoke vehicle for national-level competition alongside universities from around the world.

Mission

Highlander Racing exists to foster professional relationships with global industry leaders as we build and maintain sponsor relations and our alumni network. We apply what we learn in the classroom to real engineering challenges and grow skills that last beyond graduation.

Vision

We are committed to pushing EV technology forward and advancing toward autonomous racing. With each platform cycle we refine design and manufacturing to improve performance, reliability, and testing—integrating cutting-edge capability where it makes sense for the team and the rules.
